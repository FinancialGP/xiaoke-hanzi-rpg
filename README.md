# 汉字龙珠小侠

A kids' Chinese character learning game (static HTML/JS). Play at: https://financialgp.github.io/xiaoke-hanzi-rpg/

Mostly Chinese characters (with slow read-aloud and word practice), plus a few simple maths and English questions. Story mode, a 7-dragon-ball practice round, a character card book, and a two-player mode.

Character, pinyin, meaning and word data is based on CC-CEDICT (https://cc-cedict.org), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
Fan-made, non-commercial homage; all artwork is original SVG/emoji. Not affiliated with Dragon Ball or its owners.
